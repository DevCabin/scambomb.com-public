No. The library is designed around common web-based AI tools and basic/free usage. Availability and limits can change by provider, but you do not need an enterprise AI subscription to use the resource.
Do I need all three AI tools?
No. Each prompt may suggest a good tool for the job, but those recommendations are not requirements. Most prompts are portable and can be used in a capable general-purpose AI chat.
Is this a course?
No. There are no videos, lessons or modules. It is a reference library you open when you need to accomplish a task.
Do I need to know how to write AI prompts?
No. That's the point. The prompts are already structured. You fill in the bracketed placeholders with your business details, paste the prompt into your AI tool, then review and refine the output.

Sales, invoices, email, website copy, social media, advertising, customer service, reviews, SOPs, hiring, planning, meetings, e-commerce, spreadsheets, no-code automation, security and compliance preparation are all included.
Can I use the prompts more than once?
Yes. The resource is designed as a reusable business library. Save the outputs that work for you as your own templates and return to the original prompts whenever you need a fresh version.
Can my team use it?

Will AI always give me the right answer?
No. AI can make mistakes. The library includes safety and review reminders, and you remain responsible for verifying facts, names, dates, prices, claims, legal implications and other important details before using AI-generated output.
